# Code of conduct

Kelus is a place for students, educators, and contributors to improve source-backed learning together.

Be respectful. Discuss the work, not the person. Welcome newcomers, assume good intent, and keep feedback specific and constructive.

Do not share private student work, credentials, personal information, or copyrighted course material you do not have permission to share. Harassment, discrimination, or deliberately unsafe contributions are not welcome.

If something needs attention, contact the maintainer through the repository profile rather than escalating publicly.
