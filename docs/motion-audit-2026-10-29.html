Important

1 · On a phone, the hero demo plays before anyone can see it

Important

components/hero/LeitnerHero.module.css:46–64

The animations start on page load. At 390 px the demonstration sits below the first screen, so it has finished by the time a student scrolls to it: they meet the end state and never see a line become a question, the whole point of the hero.

Fix: start the pass when the panel is half in view (an IntersectionObserver adds data-play; the keyframes run only under it). Desktop, where it is in view on load, is unchanged.

Fix: plays once it is in view

Through Jakub's lens: motion that happens where nobody looks is cost without polish. Through Jhey's: this is the one story moment on the page; let it start on its audience.

2 · The hero's keyframes use the browser's default ease

Important

components/hero/LeitnerHero.module.css:46, 53, 62, 64

animation: kelusHeroCard 4.8s 1 both names no timing function, so every card, mark and check runs on ease, while the rest of Kelus uses cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1). The cards drift in rather than arrive.

Fix: add the Kelus curve to the mark, card, check and next-step animations (the typing keeps steps()).

Now: ease
Fix: Kelus curve

Through Emil's lens: easing is the most important part of an animation; the default lacks strength. Through Jakub's: one curve across the product is what makes it feel made by one hand.

3 · Every list staggers

Important

components/PricingReveal.tsx:53 (2 plan lists, 11 lines) · components/HowItWorks.tsx:20 (rows in 4 chapters)

Six lists across two pages arrive line by line. One staggered moment reads as care; six read as a template, and on pricing the visitor waits about a second to read a price list.

Fix: plan lines arrive with their plan, in one piece. In How it works keep the stagger only where order is the message (the plan rows in chapter 3, which reorder) and let the other chapters' rows appear with their fragment.

Now: line by line
Fix: the plan arrives as one

Through Emil's lens: utility lists get no stagger. Through Jakub's: hierarchy, not uniformity; save sequence for the one place sequence means something.

4 · Page titles rise in before they can be read

Important

app/pricing/page.tsx:39 · components/HowItWorks.tsx (hero h1)

"Start revising. Stay in control." and "Your lessons become a better revision habit." fade, rise and scale in. A title is the first thing read; moving it only delays that.

Fix: titles and ledes are simply there. The arrival moves to the illustration and the plans, which carry no reading.

Now: the title moves
Fix: the title is there; the art arrives

Through Emil's lens: animations should serve a purpose, not announce themselves. Through Jakub's: if people notice the heading's entrance, it is too much.

5 · "Play it again" snaps the demo away

Important

components/hero/HeroReplay.tsx

The replay remounts the demonstration, so the checked answer, the marks and the cards vanish in one frame before the pass restarts. The jump reads as a glitch.

Fix: fade the finished state out over 150 ms (opacity and a 4 px lift, subtler than the entrance), then remount. Reduced motion keeps the button hidden, as now.

Now: snaps
Fix: settles out, then replays

Through Jakub's lens: exits are subtler than entrances, but they exist. Through Emil's: the action is user-initiated and rare, so a short, plain transition is right.

Opportunities

A · Remove the dead <Reveal> wrappers

Opportunity

components/home/HomeVisualChapters.tsx:12, 16 (and the second chapter)

initial={false} with whileInView={{ opacity: 1 }} never animates. Either delete the wrappers (simplest, and these tiles are reading content) or let only the drawing arrive.

B · The four step tiles share one identical entrance

Opportunity

components/home/RevisionLoopVisuals.tsx:22–37

Four tiles, the same rise, 0.32 s apart, and then each has its own micro-motion. The micro-motions are the moment; the tile entrance competes with them. Let the grid appear as one and keep the four micro-motions in sequence.

C · One materialising moment in the hero

Opportunity

components/hero/LeitnerHero.module.css (kelusHeroCard)

The question cards could come into focus (opacity, 6 px rise and a 4 px blur clearing) as the line they come from is marked: one place on the site, so it stays a signal, not a template.

Working well

  • The hero plays once, holds the checked answer and offers a replay: no endless loop (WCAG 2.2.2).
  • Every motion on these pages honours prefers-reduced-motion with its finished state.
  • The step micro-motions each show the step itself (the marker marks, the blank fills, the opposite word is caught, the plan reorders).
  • Hover growth appears once (the pricing plans) instead of on every card.
  • Only transform, opacity, clip-path and background-size animate; nothing animates layout.

By lens

Through Jakub's lens: the pages are close to production polish; the gaps are consistency (one curve, findings 2) and hierarchy (one sequence per page, findings 3 and B).

Through Emil's lens: remove motion that only announces itself: the staggered lists and moving titles (3, 4), and the dead wrappers (A).

Through Jhey's lens: the hero is the place to be expressive; make sure it plays where it is seen (1) and give it one materialising beat (C).
